01 — OUR STORY

We believe
goodness is
something we do.

SAHARA is the Social Welfare Society of the IIT Madras BS Degree community — a space for willing hearts to come together and make a meaningful difference.

Through hands-on volunteering, conversations with changemakers, awareness initiatives and creative competitions, we help students connect with social causes beyond the screen.
